How Screening Works

        
    
          
  • Semantic RAG: Searches candidate resumes using vector embeddings to match context, domain experience, and skills even when words don't match literally.
    • 
          
  • RAG Fusion (Toggle): Splits multi-requirement job postings into 3–4 sub-queries and aggregates candidates using Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) to discover well-rounded candidates.
    • 
          
  • Explainable Shortlists: Groq LLM evaluates the retrieved applicants against your criteria, provides comparison matrices, and explains candidate trade-offs.
    • 
        


        
Useful Queries to Try

        
    
          
  • By Job Description: "Senior backend engineer with Go, Kafka, distributed systems, and AWS"
    • 
          
  • By Applicant ID: "Applicant ID 12" or "Tell me about candidate 154"
    • 
          
  • Follow-up Comparisons: "Compare the top 2 candidates and rank their cloud experience"
